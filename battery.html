Why Add a Battery?

Stop Paying Peak Rates. Start Owning Your Energy.

A home battery stores cheap energy and releases it when you need it most — cutting your reliance on the grid to near zero.

🌙

Use Your Solar After Dark

Without a battery, excess solar is exported for a low feed-in rate. With one, you use it yourself at night — worth 3–4× more.

🔦

Blackout Protection

Keep your lights, fridge, and essentials running during grid outages. Perfect for storm-prone SE Queensland.

📉

Dodge Peak Electricity Rates

Charge overnight on cheap off-peak tariffs and discharge during expensive peak hours (4pm–9pm). Saves hundreds per year.

🌱

Near-Zero Grid Dependence

Solar + battery can cover 80–100% of a home's energy needs. You become your own power station.

📱

Monitor From Your Phone

Modern batteries come with apps showing real-time usage, charging status, and savings. Full visibility, anywhere.

💎

Future-Proof Your Home

As electricity prices rise year on year, your battery gets more valuable. Lock in energy independence today.
