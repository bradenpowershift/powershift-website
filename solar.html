Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

After government STC rebates, a quality 6.6kW system typically costs $4,000–$7,000 installed. Larger systems (10kW+) start around $7,000–$12,000. The exact price depends on your roof type, system size, and chosen panels/inverter brand. Get a free quote to find out your exact cost.
Most homes in Queensland and WA are excellent candidates for solar — we have some of the best sun exposure in the world. The main factors are roof direction (north-facing is ideal, east/west works well too), available roof space, and shading. Your installer will assess this during the free site visit.
The federal Small-scale Technology Certificate (STC) scheme provides rebates worth $1,500–$4,000 depending on your system size and location. This is automatically deducted from your upfront price — you don't need to apply separately. Queensland also has an interest-free loan scheme for eligible households.
Most residential solar installations take 1–2 days once approved. From your initial quote to being switched on usually takes 2–6 weeks, depending on council approvals and grid connection paperwork from your energy retailer.
