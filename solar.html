Solar Questions, Answered

A quality 6.6kW system typically costs $4,500–$8,000 after STC rebates, depending on the brand and installer. Premium systems with better panels and inverters sit at the higher end but offer longer warranties and better performance. Your installer will give you an exact quote based on your roof, usage and location.
Most Australian homes are suitable. You need a roof (or section of roof) that faces north, east or west with minimal shading. Your installer will assess your roof via satellite imagery or a site visit — at no cost to you — before quoting.
The federal government's Small-scale Technology Certificate (STC) scheme reduces your upfront cost by $2,000–$4,000 depending on system size and location. Some state governments offer additional rebates. Your installer handles all the paperwork — the discount is applied at point of sale.
Most residential solar installations take one day. From signing the quote to flipping the switch, the full process (including council approval and grid connection) typically takes 2–6 weeks depending on your distributor and location.
