The Process

How Our Lead System Works

We've built the marketing machine. You plug in and install.

Step 01

We Run the Ads

We manage Meta and Google campaigns targeting homeowners in Brisbane, Gold Coast, and Perth actively searching for solar, batteries, and EV chargers.

Step 02

Homeowner Submits — With Proof

They fill in their name, phone, email, postcode, and service details. Then we go further — we request photos of their switchboard, roof, garage or install point, and any existing solar setup. If they're willing to photograph their switchboard and describe their property, they're not a tyre-kicker. That's a buyer.

Step 03

Lead Lands With You

You receive the full lead via SMS and email within 2 hours. Call them while they're still hot — that's where partners win.

Step 04

You Quote & Install

You handle the site visit, quote, and installation. We handle finding the next one. Simple as that.
